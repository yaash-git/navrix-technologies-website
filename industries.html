Context before convention

Start with the work as it is.

Sector familiarity can be useful, but it can’t replace understanding the specific people and responsibilities involved. We map the current experience, identify where the friction sits, and learn which regulations, tools, or operating realities should shape the solution.

When a request needs expertise outside the agreed team, that gap should be surfaced early so the right subject-matter support can be involved.

Questions we ask

Make the problem specific before making the product.

  • Who is trying to do what, and in which setting?
  • What information or decision is difficult today?
  • Which systems, policies, and handoffs are involved?
  • What would a useful change look like to the people affected?
  • Who will own and support the product once it is live?
